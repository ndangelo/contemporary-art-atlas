Olafur Eliasson: The Weather Project

Art Theory · 2023 · Independent artwork overview

Watch on YouTube ↗

Immersive installations that make viewers aware of perception, space, light, and collective experience.

Olafur Eliasson's The weather project (2003) transformed Tate Modern's Turbine Hall with monofrequency lighting, a mirrored ceiling, and artificial mist. The artist's studio provides detailed documentation, including materials and photographic credits. Photographs of the installation also appear on Wikimedia Commons, but a photographer's Creative Commons license does not by itself settle copyright in the underlying installation.
