Jasmine Becket-Griffith

An unusual case study in contemporary image-making and voluntary release of artwork into the public domain.

Jasmine Becket-Griffith, known for her fantasy paintings, has launched a project to release a substantial portion of her back catalogue under CC0. Her public-domain project explicitly describes 625 high-resolution scans spanning 1999–2021. The Atlas prioritizes works made from 2000 onward and treats the CC0 designation as applicable to the listed released works, not automatically to all future works or associated photographs.

Artwork image research: High-priority CC0 candidate: direct artist declaration and downloadable scans. A license statement must be linked to each actual image file before we reproduce it directly in the Atlas.
