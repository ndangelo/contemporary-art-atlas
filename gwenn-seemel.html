Gwenn Seemel

Contemporary portraiture, identity, public engagement, and the politics of artistic ownership.

Gwenn Seemel is a contemporary painter whose expressive portraits and public-facing projects explore identity, representation, and relationships between artist and audience. The artist states that work produced since 2003 has been released directly into the public domain.
