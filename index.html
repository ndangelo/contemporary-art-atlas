About

A research archive in progress

Profiles are original introductory summaries, not substitutes for primary sources. References are presented in both APA 7 and Chicago Notes and Bibliography styles where supported. Artist cards provide both formats with copy controls; long-form profiles are being updated. Verify source metadata and exact wording before scholarly quotation. Copyrighted artwork images are not reproduced without permission; follow linked institutional sources to view work.
